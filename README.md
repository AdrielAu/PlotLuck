# Plotluck

Where in London should your restaurant open? Plotluck scores every London postcode district for how likely a restaurant of a given cuisine is to survive there, to help small independents and under-represented cuisines find their spot.

Built for the IBM Z Datathon 2026 (Community Impact): a hybrid of two neural networks trained on IBM LinuxONE, served as a static map in the browser.
