# Train on Tripadvisor-derived data, publish only model outputs

On 2026-10-17 the team chose to train NN1 on the Kaggle "TripAdvisor European restaurants" dataset (stefanoleone992, v1, 2021), even though its uploader-declared CC0 does not show that Tripadvisor granted downstream rights, and [Tripadvisor's UK terms](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/uk-terms-of-use) restrict copying and redistribution. It is the only source with review fields that could deliver the MVP by its deadline. Plotluck is non-commercial research, so the rows are used only for computational analysis, and only what the model produces is published, never what the source holds. This is a risk the team accepts, not legal advice.

The rules that follow:

- The full CSV lives only on LinuxONE (`data/full/`). No laptop or temporary copies.
- No raw rows are committed anywhere. The committed restaurant sample is synthetic, generated by a seeded script with the full file's schema.
- The site and the submission show model outputs only: weights, district scores, the ranked district list, and the cuisine and trait vocabulary. They never show restaurant names, locations, ratings or review counts, or district aggregates of raw Tripadvisor fields.
- One exception: NN1 needs restaurant supply as a browser input, so the site may publish per-district counts of restaurants, in total and per cuisine group. Aggregates of ratings or review counts stay private. Agreed by the team during [dataset acquisition](https://github.com/AdrielAu/PlotLuck/issues/43); it is a team publication decision, not a grant of source rights.
- The site, README and submission credit the dataset and say Plotluck is not affiliated with Tripadvisor.
- The decision holds only while Plotluck stays non-commercial (no ads, paid tier, or sale or licensing of the weights or scores). Commercial use requires retraining on a permitted source first.

## Considered options

- **Foursquare OS Places (Apache-2.0) with a survival label**: permitted and records closures, but it has no ratings, overturns ADR 0002 and could not be ready for the MVP. It stays the candidate replacement after the MVP.
- **Asking Tripadvisor for permission first**: no answer would arrive before the deadline.

Decided in [Choose a permitted restaurant-review training source](https://github.com/AdrielAu/PlotLuck/issues/46).
